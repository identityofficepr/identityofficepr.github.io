Lifestyle.

The things we choose are part of the stories we tell.

Lifestyle is not a shopping list. It is a language of objects, rituals, places, communities and desires. We are interested in what those choices communicate and in the people and labour that give them meaning.

This department considers fashion as identity, luxury as both craft and commerce, and everyday style as an evolving cultural record. Our stories favour specificity over trend labels and curiosity over easy aspiration.
