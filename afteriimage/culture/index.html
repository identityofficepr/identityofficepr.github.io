Pop Culture.

The spotlight changes everything.

Pop culture gives us something irresistible: a public story in which we can see the clothes, hear the soundtrack and argue about the ending. A red-carpet debut can become a cultural event. A stranger's girlfriend can become the centre of a fan debate. And a single carefully shared photograph can travel farther than the people inside it intended.

Our coverage begins with public, verified information and examines what the reaction says about attention, identity and the audience's sense of access. We do not claim to know anyone's private motivations, and we do not publish unverified cheating allegations. There is plenty worth talking about without making it up.
