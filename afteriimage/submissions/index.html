Write. Publish. Participate. Earn.

Our contributor revenue programme is designed for writers who consistently publish strong work and help it reach an audience.

To qualify, publish at least three approved articles, each reaching a minimum of 200 verified views, and actively support the promotion of your work through appropriate channels. A combined 600 views is not sufficient unless each of the three articles individually reaches 200.

Once eligibility is verified and participation terms are agreed, contributors may receive 30% of eligible net advertising revenue attributable to their qualifying articles from the date of approval onward. AFTERIIMAGE. retains 70% to support editorial operations and publication costs. Earlier revenue is not paid retroactively.

Promotion is expected, but contributors are not required to spend money on advertising. Artificial or manipulated traffic does not count. Views alone do not guarantee advertising earnings.

Revenue sharing is subject to operational availability, reliable article-level revenue attribution, written contributor terms and verified earnings. No payments are promised before the programme is activated and a contributor agreement is accepted. Compensation for any separately commissioned work must be agreed in writing beforehand.

Statements & payments.

Eligible contributors will receive a weekly earnings statement by email when reporting data is available. Statements will outline verified article views, eligible advertising revenue attributed to each article, the contributor's 30% share, adjustments where applicable, and the balance awaiting payment. Weekly statements are informational estimates, not payment confirmations.

Payments are reviewed and approved manually by AFTERIIMAGE. Approved payouts may be sent through PayPal, subject to availability in the contributor's country, account verification, applicable fees, and written payment instructions. Contributors should not send PayPal credentials; payment details will be requested securely only when needed. The proposed minimum payout threshold is R250 in finalised eligible earnings. Balances below the threshold carry forward.

AFTERIIMAGE. retains editorial and administrative discretion over acceptance, verification, eligibility, fraud prevention, corrections and payment approval, exercised reasonably and in accordance with the contributor agreement and applicable law. No earnings or payout is guaranteed. We may withhold disputed or invalid earnings while reviewing them and will communicate material adjustments to the contributor.

Weekly statement delivery, article-level revenue attribution and PayPal disbursements require operational setup. These features will commence only after the programme is activated; this page does not itself create automated emails or process payments.

Editorial standards.

We prioritise originality, accuracy, research, distinctive perspective and responsible reporting. Contributors retain authorship credit; publication permissions, editing rights and any payment terms will be confirmed before publication. Editorial acceptance and monetisation eligibility are separate decisions.
