AFTERIIMAGE. is an editorial publication examining the people, ideas and cultural moments that shape how we see the world.

01 / OUR POINT OF VIEW

Culture leaves an impression.

An image may capture attention. A story gives it meaning. We look beyond the surface to explore the decisions, conversations and cultural forces that make people and ideas memorable.

AFTERIIMAGE. publishes interviews, commentary and analysis across music, fashion, lifestyle, entertainment and creative business. Our signature series, THE ANATOMY OF., examines the mechanics of identity, influence and cultural positioning.

02 / WHY WE EXIST

An editorial extension of perception.

AFTERIIMAGE. was established as the editorial arm of THE IDENTITY OFFICE™, a public relations and brand strategy firm working at the intersection of identity, image and culture.

Where the firm helps artists, creators and brands develop their narratives and public presence, the publication examines the cultural landscape in which those narratives are received. The relationship is rooted in a shared belief: understanding perception matters as much as shaping it.

The publication has its own editorial purpose. Coverage is not a client service, and any relevant commercial or client relationship will be identified transparently.
