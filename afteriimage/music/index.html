AFTERIIMAGE. / DEPARTMENT / MUSIC

Music.

Music is never only the sound. It is the world built around it.

Music creates its own identities before the industry gives those identities a name. We examine the records, performances, scenes and structures that change the conversation: who receives recognition, which sounds cross between communities and why certain artists remain unforgettable long after a release cycle ends.

Our coverage combines reported developments with clear editorial interpretation. The subject may be an awards ballot, a new sound or the economics of making a living from the work. The question remains the same: what is this moment telling us about culture?
