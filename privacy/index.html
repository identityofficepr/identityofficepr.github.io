How THE IDENTITY OFFICE™ handles personal information.

Privacy noticeUPDATED 11 NOVEMBER 2026

Information we collect

When you contact us, you may provide your name, email address and details about your project. Our enquiry form opens your email application; it does not submit information to a website database. We receive information only if you send the resulting email.

How information is used

We use enquiries to respond, assess potential engagements, communicate about services and maintain necessary business records. We do not sell enquiry information.

Hosting and third parties

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The site also requests web fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may process technical information, such as IP addresses, under their own policies. Email correspondence is handled by the relevant email providers. Advertising services on ANATOMY articles are provided by Google AdSense when permitted.

Cookies and preferences

We use local browser storage to remember your cookie preference. Display advertisements from Google AdSense may appear on articles in our ANATOMY editorial section only. We load the advertising service after you select Accept for optional cookies. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies and process data such as IP address and browsing activity for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. You can change your preference on our Cookie policy page. See Google's advertising privacy information for further details.

Retention and rights

We keep correspondence only as reasonably necessary for enquiries, engagements, legal obligations and legitimate business needs. You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law, by emailing identityofficepr@gmail.com.
