SĒAN MOSIKILI is a South African singer, songwriter and producer blending contemporary pop, alternative R&B and electronic music. His debut EP LOST LOVER’S AVENUE arrived in June 2026, followed by GENIE (IBIZA SUNSET REMIX) and MONACO. His artist identity connects atmospheric sound, emotionally precise storytelling and evolving visual worlds.
