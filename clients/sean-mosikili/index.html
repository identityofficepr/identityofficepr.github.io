THE ROSTER / Recording artist / Singer · Songwriter · Record Producer

SĒAN
MOSIKILI

SĒAN MOSIKILI (born Tswelopele Sean Mosikili, April 11, 2005) is a South African-born singer, songwriter and producer blending contemporary pop, alternative R&B and electronic music. His debut EP LOST LOVER’S AVENUE arrived in June 2026, followed by GENIE (IBIZA SUNSET REMIX) and MONACO and a rerelease eedition of ALL MINE on the 14th of November 2026. His artist identity connects atmospheric sound, emotionally precise storytelling and evolving visual worlds often referencing A24 films.
